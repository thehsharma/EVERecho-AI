10  The demonstration archive

The seed builds a complete, synthetic archive for one invented person: Kamala Deshpande. Six recorded sessions, one scanned letter, a family around her, and one deliberate imperfection.

pnpm db:seed creates six audio sessions (session-01-childhood, -02-school, -03-work, -04-family, -05-money, -06-partition), one document (letter-1974-transfer.txt), the people and places they mention, and the memories derived from them. It then runs the real ingestion pipeline over that material — the same handlers a production upload goes through — so what you browse is genuinely derived, not pre-written rows.

Everything here is invented

No real personal information is bundled with this repository. The names, dates, places, recordings and the letter are all fabricated for demonstration, which is stated in the seed file itself and on the demo page in the product.
