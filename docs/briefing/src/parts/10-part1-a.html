Where you actually are

Four pieces of ground truth. Read this part once, carefully, before you act on anything in the rest of the document.

1  The honest position

You have built something most funded startups do not have: a complete, tested, running product with the hard parts genuinely finished. You have not yet built a company, and the gap between those two things is larger than it feels from inside the code.

What is real VERIFIED

All of this can be checked by anyone with the repository, today:

A running application, end to end3 apps, 7 packages, ~25,000 lines
Database schema with enforced isolation50 tables, 28 under forced RLS
Versioned API, generated from its own validators70 operations
Screens33 routes
Tests that actually ran193 unit and integration, no mocks
Browser and accessibility54 tests, 0 WCAG 2.2 AA violations
AI evaluation32/32, all four blocking targets met
Documentation15 documents, plus a 57-page product PDF

That is a genuinely strong engineering position, and it is worth being precise about why: the parts you finished are the parts that are normally faked. Consent, provenance, isolation and deletion are usually a policy page and a hope. Here they are a pure authorisation function, forced row-level security, a verification step that drops unsupported sentences, and a resumable deletion that issues a receipt. That work is real and it is not easily copied.
