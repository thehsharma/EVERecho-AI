7  The launch gate

One page. Print it. No real person's memories enter the system until every box is ticked and you have signed the bottom. If a box cannot be ticked, the answer is to fix it, not to add a caveat.

Legal and accountability

  • Counsel has answered the §5 brief, and the consent copy, teach-back wording, terms and succession screen carry their approval
  • A policy exists for third parties named inside memories, and it is implemented, not merely written
  • An accountable person is named by name in the runbook, on the support page, and in the customer agreement
  • The name is cleared, or a rename has been completed
  • Nothing on the public site claims traction, customers or capability that does not exist

Security

  • Production authentication works: no AUTH_DRIVER=local, MFA available, password reset working
  • Every development secret has been replaced and lives in a secret manager
  • Encryption at rest with a customer-managed key
  • A real malware scanner is in the upload path
  • An independent security review has been completed and its findings closed or explicitly accepted in writing

Operations

  • Backups run, and a restore has been performed into a clean environment and timed
  • A deletion has been performed end to end and verified against the database afterwards — not against the receipt
  • Monitoring and alerting reach a human within minutes; that human has agreed to be reached
  • Logs, error reports and support views have been inspected and contain no memory content
  • All eight integrations have been executed, not merely deployed
  • Billing works, including verified webhook signatures, and a refund has been tested

Product

  • An accessibility review with disabled users has been completed and blocking findings fixed
  • The interview capture path has been exercised by hand, on real hardware, by someone over seventy
  • Export has been produced and opened without EverEcho present
  • A written runbook exists for the death of a storyteller during the pilot (§18)
  • Real unit costs are known, and the price covers them

8  Horizon 2: the first ten families

Horizon 2 Ten paying archives. Not a hundred. Not free ones. Months 2–5

The purpose of this cohort is not revenue and not growth. It is to answer four questions that no amount of thinking will answer, and to produce the one asset that makes everything in Part IV easier.

The four questions

Question How you will know
Q1 Will anyone pay, at a price that works? Ten families pay the §5 provisional price without a discount. If you had to discount, the answer is no and you have learned it cheaply.
Q2 Will the storyteller actually do the work? Payment → consent → first recording → first approved memory. Measure the drop at each step. This is the split-incentive problem from §3 meeting reality.
Q3 What does one archive really cost to serve? Every hour you spend, logged. Every provider invoice, attributed. Fill in the §2 worksheet with real numbers.
Q4 Does the refusal actually sell, or merely survive? Watch what people react to. If nobody mentions the citations or the abstention, your positioning is a private conviction rather than a market position — and §10 changes.

How to find ten families

Do not run advertising. At ten units, paid acquisition teaches you nothing and costs you focus. Go where the occasion already lives (§3, problem 3):

  1. Your own circle first, but paying. Friends and family who pay full price are a real signal; ones you comp are a favour, and favours produce polite feedback that will mislead you for months.
  2. One channel partner, unpaid pilot. A single estate planner, hospice, senior-living director or community organiser who will introduce you to three or four families. One partner, not five — you are testing whether the channel works, not building a channel programme.
  3. A community with a reason. Diaspora and migration communities have an unusually strong version of this need: the generation that moved is ageing, and the story of why they moved is exactly what is lost first. Your own demonstration archive is built around precisely that. INFERENCE
  4. Do the interviews yourself. All ten. This is the concierge phase from §2 and it is deliberately unscalable. You are writing the specification for what the product must eventually automate, and you cannot write it from a desk.
Two rules for this cohort

Nobody gets it free. A free archive answers none of the four questions.
Nobody gets a discount to close. A discount converts Q1 from a measurement into a wish.

What would falsify the idea

Decide these thresholds now, before you are emotionally invested in ten specific families. Write them down and date them.

Fewer than 5 of 10 reach a first approved memorythe split-incentive problem is fatal as designed
Cost to serve exceeds price, and is not obviously automatablethe business does not work at this price
Nobody mentions citation or refusal, unpromptedreposition; the wedge is yours, not theirs
You cannot recruit 10 without paid acquisitionthe channel thesis in §10 is wrong

None of these means stop. Each means change something specific, and knowing which is worth more than the ten customers.
