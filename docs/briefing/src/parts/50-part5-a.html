Governance and risk

How to make the refusal survive you, what will actually go wrong, and the conditions under which I would tell you to stop.

17  Binding the refusal so it survives you

Today the prohibitions hold because you wrote them and you care. That is the weakest form of guarantee there is, and the whole product's value rests on it.

Why this is urgent rather than philosophical

The pressure to add the voice will not arrive as a villain. It will arrive as a good person with a good argument at a bad moment: a board member after two flat quarters, an acquirer whose offer changes your family's life, a large customer who will sign if you add just one feature, or a grieving user who begs you for it. Every one of those is a sincere request, and the reason to decide now is that you will not be at your most clear-headed in any of those rooms.

You already did the technical half: a policy compiler that refuses, a function that discards first-person output, database constraints, and tests that fail if any of it is removed. VERIFIED But code is changed by whoever controls the company, and right now that protection is exactly as strong as your continued ownership.

The instruments, from cheapest to strongest

InstrumentWhat it doesCost
1Publish the prohibitions as termsMakes them a promise every customer can hold you to, not a marketing claim. Enforceable by the people they protect.A page and counsel's time. Do this before the first customer.
2A purpose clause in the constitutional documentsNames the prohibited activities in the company's own governing document, so removing them is a formal act with a paper trail rather than a product decision.Incorporation-time work. Far harder to add later.
3Reserved mattersChanging the prohibitions requires a supermajority or a specific consent, so no ordinary board majority can do it quietly.Must be negotiated before you take money, which is why §14 says choose investors on this.
4An outside voice with a real vetoOne or two independent people — an ethicist, a palliative-care clinician, an archivist — with a defined and narrow veto over the prohibited list. Not an advisory board; advisory boards advise.Modest fees, and the discipline of being argued with.
5Change-of-control protectionsOn acquisition, the prohibitions survive, and every family gets notice plus a guaranteed window to export and delete. It makes the archives less useful to a buyer who wants them for the wrong reason — which is the point.Real. It may reduce your exit price, and that is the trade you are choosing.
6Publish the enforcing testsAnyone can verify the refusal is still enforced in the shipped product. Turns a claim into something falsifiable by strangers.Nearly free. Uniquely credible.
Decision — how far to go

Recommendation: do 1, 2 and 6 now — before the first customer and before incorporation is final, because two of them are much harder to retrofit. Negotiate 3 and 5 into the first round in §14. Add 4 at stage 1, when there are real families whose interests deserve representation that is not you.

Instrument 5 is the one that will feel expensive, so be clear-eyed: you are deliberately lowering the value of an exit to a buyer whose plan is to remove the refusal. That is not a sacrifice made against your interest — it is the thing that makes the promise real, and the promise is the company.
