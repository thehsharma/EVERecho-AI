How to use this

A chief of staff does three things: keeps the sequence honest, makes sure decisions actually get made, and says the thing nobody else will say. This document does all three, in that order.

The evidence discipline carries over

The build refused to state anything it could not support. A plan written to a looser standard than the product would be worse than useless, so every consequential claim here is labelled:

VERIFIED Established by running the code or reading the repository. You can check it yourself today.
INFERENCE My reasoning from things that are verified. Should hold, but it is an argument, not an observation.
ASSUMPTION A number or belief I have adopted so the plan can proceed. You must replace these with real ones.
UNKNOWN I do not know, and neither does anyone until it is measured. These are the research tasks.

There are no market-size figures in this document, no competitor funding amounts, and no citations to reports I have not read. Where you need those, they are named as research tasks with the question stated precisely. A plan built on invented numbers fails in exactly the way this product refuses to.
